Most tracking systems collapse under too many metrics. Here's how to choose the handful that matter.

The instinct when building a dashboard is to track everything measurable. In practice, a dashboard with thirty metrics gets checked by no one after month two. The teams that keep their systems alive pick five to eight numbers that map directly to the decisions leadership actually makes week to week — and retire everything else.

Start narrow. You can always add a metric back later if a real decision is waiting on it.
