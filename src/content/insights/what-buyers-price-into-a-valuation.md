It's rarely revenue alone. Predictability, documented systems, and leadership depth move the multiple more than growth rate does.

Buyers and lenders discount anything they can't verify. A business that can show three years of clean, consistent reporting — and prove it doesn't collapse the moment the founder steps back — earns a materially higher multiple than one growing faster but running on instinct.

This is why the work Altaris does — installing KPI systems, leadership cadence, and documented process — isn't separate from valuation. It *is* valuation work, whether or not a sale is on the table.
