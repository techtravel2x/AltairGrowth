A simple way to see how many decisions in your business still can't happen without you in the room.

For one week, keep a running list of every decision that landed on your desk — not because you wanted it to, but because no one else was positioned to make it. At the end of the week, sort the list into two piles: decisions that genuinely needed your judgment, and decisions that only needed you because no one else had the authority, information, or confidence to make the call.

That second pile is your founder-dependency number. It's usually bigger than people expect — and it's the clearest map of where to build structure next.
